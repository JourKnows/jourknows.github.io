Nagpaabot ng pakikiramay si Pangulong Ferdinand "Bongbong" Marcos Jr. (PBBM) kay King Maha Vajiralongkorn, sa Royal Family, at sa mamamayan ng Thailand kasunod ng pagpanaw ni Princess Bajrakitiyabha Narendira Debyavati.

Pumanaw si Princess Bajrakitiyabha, anak ni King Maha Vajiralongkorn, sa edad na 47 dahil sa multiple health problems at halos apat na taong nasa coma.

"On behalf of the Government of the Republic of the Philippines and the Filipino people, I extend my deepest condolences to His Majesty King Maha Vajiralongkorn, the Royal Family, and the people of Thailand on the passing of Her Royal Highness Princess Bajrakitiyabha Narendira Debyavati," saad ni Marcos.

"Her lifelong dedication to public service and her commitment to justice, human dignity and women's rights leave an enduring legacy that will be remembered by the Thai nation and admired by the international community," dagdag pa niya.

"The Philippines stands in solidarity with the people of Thailand in mourning this profound loss and in honoring the remarkable legacy of Her Royal Highness."

Batay sa mga ulat, naospital si Princess Bajrakitiyabha noong Disyembre 2022 matapos mawalan ng malay habang nagsasanay ng mga aso para sa isang army exhibition. 

Ayon sa palasyo, nagkaroon siya ng mycoplasma infection, isang uri ng bacterial infection na karaniwang kaugnay sa pulmonya.

“Her Royal Highness’s dedication, compassion and love for the people will forever be remembered with gratitude,” saad ng Thai Government sa isang pagpupugay sa yumaong prinsesa.
