Across different seasons, Pinoy Big Brother has introduced Filipinos coming from different stories, backgrounds, and communities. Inside, challenges test their grit while the outside world recognizes the perseverance and personality of every housemate.

As Kuya’s house opens once more for the 3rd sequel of its Collab Edition, 16 housemates, comprising eight Kapamilyas and eight Kapusos, will live under one roof, completely cut off from the outside world.

***KAPAMILYA HOUSEMATE LINEUP***

**Ang Rampanalong Apo ng Pampanga**

From his early TV recognition to his runway journey, Calix Esguerra transitions to mainstream TV as he lives inside Kuya’s house as an official housemate, walking his Kapampangan roots within Kuya’s halls for the outside world to see.

18-year-old Calix debuted in the entertainment industry in early 2025, particularly in TV5’s idol survival reality show *Be The NEXT: 9 Dreamers.* Over time, he expanded his career to male pageantry, winning the title of Mister Teen of the Universe Philippines 2025, which earned his prominence and opened his modeling journey, walking in runway stages like Bench Body Fashion Show. For Calix, keeping the model-body facade takes a strict 5-day-a-week workout, securing 8 hours of sleep, and hitting his daily protein targets.



**Ang Madlang Babe ng Antipolo**

From Sexy Babe to Madlang Babe! Cianne Dominguez from Antipolo is ready to show the outside world her wit, beauty, and natural charm entering Kuya’s house with full determination ready to transform every spotlight into pure magic with her unmatchable personality.

25-year-old Cianne began to emerge in the music industry, releasing pop singles including “Sumpa” and “Traffic” signed under the StarPop label. On major ABS-CBN programs like *ASAP Natin ‘To* and *Magandang Buhay*, she expanded her television image with her guest appearance. But before music and guestings, the people knew her as a Sexy Babe contestant for It’s Showtime’s segment *Sexy Babe.* With her charm and chemistry with the hosts, she was eventually regularized as a co-host on *It’s Showtime.* In Cianne’s ABS-CBN “Celebrity Slambook”, she hopes to star in a musical play and dreams of taking on the international Miss Universe stage. 



**Ang Darling K-Pop Idol ng Las Piñas**

Gehlee Dangca bloomed in Korea, representing the Philippines with pride. Travelling back to her home country to unveil another journey inside Kuya’s house, this time, she carries the name of her hometown: Las Piñas.

19-year-old Gehlee began her career at age 12 working as a fashion model and competing in beauty pageants in the Philippines, but it wasn’t until 2023 when she joined SBS’s South Korean reality survival show Universe Ticket, where she secured her spot in the 8-member girl group *UNIS*. For two and a half years, she lived the Korean idol life, but returned to the Philippines to fulfill a new chapter after *UNIS* concluded its contract. With her return, she released her English solo debut single *Closer**.*** In Idol Q&A segments, she shared that spicy tteokbokki is her go-to Korean food, and is a big fan of strawberry-flavored treats.



**Ang Adventure Boy-Next-Door ng Palawan**

As a prominent travel and lifestyle content creator, Jeremiah “Jeo” Ong from Palawan enters Kuya’s roof and bids temporary farewell to the outside world that he has known his whole life. 

18-year-old Jeo is one of the core members of the Ong family. His family’s digital platform focuses on outdoor activities and eco-adventures, showcasing Palawan and their hometown’s local destinations. With Jeo’s family by his side, he completed his Senior High School education at Southville International School Affiliated with Foreign Universities in mid-2026. Having to complete their Palawan venture, they aim to do the same with the whole Philippine archipelago leaving no soil untouched by their footprints.

**Ang Hustling Swag Stunner ng Cebu**

The fashion industry is nothing without Nathan Garcia’s swag. Rooted in Cebu, Nathan pursues another journey as he carries his unmatchable style inside the PBB show.

18-year-old Nathan worked on various jobs to provide for his family when his parents separated. He took on jobs such as being a water fetcher and a garbage collector, and caught bigger responsibilities after his father went to jail because of a family misunderstanding. Since then, he started to explore digital modeling and social media brand collaborations. Slowly in his journey, his followers rose with his content showcasing urban street fashion and music style. Scouted by a talent manager with his online work, he signed under ABS-CBN and made his acting debut in local teleseryes.



**Ang Kwelang Creator ng Taguig** 

The boredom fades inside the house if Primo Alterado joins the club. Raised in Taguig City, he grew online as the playful, talkative, and friendly person he is. This time, he carries that attitude to share joy to his fellow housemates.

20-year-old Luis Primo Alterado began his career in 2020 with his relatable everyday comedy content. With lifestyle vlogs, the camera became a way for self-expression. Today, he has built a growing fan base across platforms as an online personality ready to make a person smile and laugh. Despite his upbeat personality, Primo came from a family with broken smiles, yet he never stopped reaching for the bright side of life and only fueled himself to work harder for him and his two siblings.  


**Ang Dedicated Son ng Pasig**

For Robbie Jaworskie from Pasig, dedication comes with real action. With his natural charm, hospitable nature, and as the eldest son of his family, he is ready to show the outside world the things he is capable of. 

26-year-old Robbie earned a degree in Management from Ateneo de Manila University and later worked in the Hospitality Industry and Business Management. As the son of Pasig City Vice Mayor Robert "Dodot" Jaworski Jr. and former actress Mikee Cojuangco-Jaworski, he gets his grounded demeanor and athletic genes, even playing as a varsity basketball player  before an injury cuts his sports career short. Throughout Robbie's journey, he signed under ABS-CBN Star Magic in 2024, with that, he debuted as Lester in the Star Cinema Film *Meet, Greet, & Bye* and made notable TV appearances as an actor, and a host in mainstream television. 



**Ang Miss Ma-Beauting Anak ng Rizal**

Beauty and brains, Shea Valerio from Rizal takes her pride from these two things. Grounded in family values, she aims to carry a good attitude inside the house, fully focused to start a new story in her life.

20-year-old Shea Valerio is a strong academic student who has consistently shown up for youth advocacy programs. With her poise and public speaking skills, she joined local beauty pageants which helped her gain fame, particularly when she was crowned Binibining Rizal 2025 Tourism. Shea also worked as a commercial model for local fashion and beauty brands, and later signed with ABS-CBN Star Magic as a talent. In her everyday endeavors, he carries his father’s advice which is to never date before earning a degree.



***KAPUSO HOUSEMATE LINEUP***

**Ang Total Dream Heartthrob ng U.S.A.** 

Known for his charming looks and gentlemanly gestures, Anthony Constantino is the living proof that a Fil-Am boy from California can capture the hearts of Filipinos back home. Born and raised in Los Angeles to his Filipino parents, he is widely known as the country’s TDH (Tall, Dark, and Handsome), a term popularized by former PBB housemate Shuvee Etrata.

24-year-old Anthony graduated from California Lutheran University with a dual degree in Computer Information System and Business Administration major in Marketing. Interestingly, Anthony’s journey into the entertainment industry was far from his original plans. He said that he initially envisioned himself working in the technology sector and even completed internships before deciding to pursue modeling in the Philippines. Nevertheless, he eventually found his calling when he signed with the Otto Models agency in Los Angeles in 2022. Later on, when he moved to the Philippines and signed as an artist under Sparkle, he starred in “Huwag Kang Titingin” and “From Vlog to Love”. Now, the confident yet down-to-earth heartthrob is ready to show that his appeal goes beyond his looks.

 

**Ang Overseas Factory Senpai ng Cavite** 

Hardworking and fearless, Bela Arcal from Cavite proves that life is truly built through sacrifice and determination. Unlike her fellow housemates, she didn’t enter showbiz at an early age. Now, she enters the house with a story to tell and an unyielding fighting spirit.

35-year-old Bela started her journey as a sales clerk in a local mall, but her determination and drive to pursue better opportunities led her to take a leap of faith. Despite her humble beginnings, she is actually a degree holder in Tourism Management. She eventually ventured beyond her field of study and migrated to Japan to work as a factory worker in hopes of a better life and a better future. Her courage to explore unfamiliar paths and embrace life’s challenges earned her the title “Senpai” among her fellow overseas Filipino workers. With her tough yet caring personality and resilience, she is set to show how determination can take a person far.



**Ang Sporty Palangga ng Bacolod**

Ethan Benares takes great pride in his roots from Negros Occidental with his goal-oriented and disciplined mindset wherever he goes. With his warm smile and love for outdoors, he is ready to swoon the audience and bring his athletic spirit into the PBB house.

18-year-old Ethan is a proud Psychology student-athlete from University of Saint La Salle. He first caught the public’s attention through GMA’s Sparkle Campus Cutie in which he was known as the “Morenong Beach Boy”. While sports and outdoor adventures are his greatest interests, he also has an unexpected favorite subject: Mathematics. Although it may not be everyone’s cup of tea, Ethan finds solving mathematical equations therapeutic, especially because of the satisfaction he gets from answering it right. Beyond sports and academics, he carries a deep passion for camping and water sports, making him the rightful owner for the title “Palangga” he proudly carries from his beloved hometown.



**Ang VetMed Content Creator ng Cavite**  

Recognized for her signature intro “This is Laaanssss!”, Lans Nagal turns every concept into a fun content that everyone can enjoy. She is one of the most recognizable digital personalities in the Kapuso lineup with an estimated 10 million followers across different social media platforms.

24-year-old Lans is a passionate and hardworking veterinary medicine student from Cavite State University. She created a lasting impact on the social media community by presenting educational topics in an engaging and accessible way. Her witty and informative viral videos even earned the stamp of approval from ABS-CBN’S TV Host, Kim Atienza. However, her skills extend beyond the digital world. Before her remarkable appearance as a content creator, Lans was already pursuing her passion as a competitive swimmer, beginning her journey as a varsity athlete during her junior high school years. She was also an active member of CSU’S Central State Government, starting as a delegate-member before eventually rising to the position of Senate President. Now, she is bound to bring the same enthusiasm inside the Bahay ni Kuya, sharing her love for learning with her fellow housemates.



**Ang Independent Archi-tista ng Manila** 

Sang'gre Lira in the fantaseryang Encantadia transitions in a new dimension, with a diamond of identity that attracts anyone, the one and only Mikee Quintos from Manila is there to shine not just as a well-known artist and singer, but as a selfless architect.

28-year-old Mikee pursued her architecture degree at the University of Santo Tomas while building her career in show business. Best known for her iconic role as Lira in GMA Network's Encantadia, she balanced taping schedules with her academic journey for 10 years before finally graduating in 2025. A true Kapuso talent, she proved that dedication makes it possible to chase two dreams at once—now stepping into Kuya's house ready to build new memories on her own.

Aside from being a licensed architect, Mikee is also the youngest of four musically-inclined siblings—her sisters are also singers and actresses, yet she’s the only one in the family who pursued a profession outside of entertainment. She actually started taking architecture even before she auditioned for Encantadia, making it her quiet personal goal long before fame.

 

**Ang Pretty Dependable Ate ng Bohol** 

Half-Filipina and half-English, Nicole Fortuna from Bohol brings warmth and gentle care wherever she goes. Growing up in England before making Bohol her home, she now carries the island’s peaceful spirit with her, ready to nurture and look out for everyone inside Kuya’s house. 

24-year-old Nicole is a content creator under Status by Sparkle who shares her island life, travels, and family moments on social media. A caring soul at heart, she has rescued and cared for numerous animals, including 11 aspins and four cats. She enters PBB not just to share her life, but to deepen her connection with her Filipino heritage and learn more about the culture her family loves.

Nicole’s family originally moved back to the Philippines because her mom wanted her and her siblings to grow up close to their Filipino roots—she didn’t speak fluent Tagalog when they first arrived, and taught herself by watching Filipino teleseryes and talking to locals in their neighborhood.



**Ang Brusko Boksingero ng Las Piñas** 

Beyond his tough exterior and quiet strength, Raheel Bhyria from Las Piñas fights not just in the ring but for everything that matters to him. Disciplined, dedicated, and deeply proud of his roots, he enters Kuya’s house ready to show that true strength comes not from anger but from perseverance and a grateful heart.

23-year-old Raheel is a Filipino-Pakistani actor and model under Sparkle GMA Artist Center. First discovered through social media, he appeared in teleseryes such as Luv Is: Caught in His Arms, Abot-Kamay na Pangarap, and Kamao. An amateur boxer, he sees the sport not merely as competition but as a way of life that has taught him focus and discipline—values he learned from his father, who worked hard to build a life for their family in the Philippines. 

Raheel was actually studying Civil Engineering before showbiz called him—he put his studies on hold to pursue acting but still hopes to finish it someday. He also grew up helping his family run their small convenience store, where he learned to interact with all kinds of people at a young age.  


**Ang Reyna Mama ng Parañaque** 

With grace that commands respect and a warmth that feels like home, Winwyn Marquez from Parañaque steps into Kuya’s house not just as a queen, but as a mother—ready to guide, listen, and care for everyone who becomes family within these walls.

33-year-old Winwyn is an actress, beauty queen, and Philippine Navy Reserve Cadet. Daughter of showbiz veterans Joey Marquez and Alma Moreno, she made history as the first Filipina to win Reina Hispanoamericana Universe Philippines 2025. Beyond her achievements on stage and screen, nothing compares to her greatest role yet—being a loving mother to her four-year-old daughter, Luna. 

Before she became a beauty queen, Winwyn actually worked behind the scenes in production and events—she didn’t always see herself in the spotlight and joined pageants later than most. She’s also a certified scuba diver and passionate environmental advocate, often using her travels to marine sanctuaries to promote ocean conservation.
