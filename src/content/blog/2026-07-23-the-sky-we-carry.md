***SUNNY***  
  
***S**ome souls arrive like morning after rain,*  
***U**ndoing grief with nothing but a bright smile.*  
***N**ear them, the weight of yesterday grows light,*

***N**o sorrow seems too great to bear awhile,*

***Y**et even the most glowing heart must rest from shining.*  
  
***RAINY***  
  
**R**ain falls as tears upon a weary face,  
**A**nd washes words too painful to be said.  
**I**n silent hours, old wounds begin to speak,  
**N**o hand can stop the thoughts that fill the head,  
**Y**et every shower leaves this earth renewed.  
  
***CLOUDY***  
  
**C**louds drift where certainty once stood,  
**L**eaving the heart to wander without a guide.  
**O**ne longs for answers hidden from the eye,  
**U**nder a sky where doubt and hope abide,  
**Y**et still the soul moves onward through the haze.  
  
***STORMY***  
  
**S**torms rise when grief and anger share one throne,  
**T**hunder speaks the words the lips conceal.  
**O**ver the heart fierce winds wage their war,  
**R**eminding us how deeply we can feel.  
**M**any break beneath such raging skies,  
**Y**et many rise more steadfast than before.
