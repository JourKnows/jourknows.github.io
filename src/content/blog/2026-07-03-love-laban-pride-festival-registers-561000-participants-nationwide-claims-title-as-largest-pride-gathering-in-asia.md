The annual Love Laban Pride Festival officially secured its position as the largest Pride celebration in Asia after drawing a record-breaking collective crowd of 561,000 participants nationwide during its synchronized events, according to Pride PH.

Organized under the comprehensive #OnePridePH campaign banner, the massive mobilization saw hundreds of thousands of LGBTQIA+ community members and their allies march simultaneously across Luzon, Visayas, and Mindanao to advocate for institutional equality.

The primary human convergence centered at the University of the Philippines (UP) Diliman campus in Quezon City, where local authorities and event security coordinators managed a flagship crowd of approximately 300,000 attendees.

In an official public statement celebrating the historic milestone, Pride PH expressed profound gratitude to the massive crowd, stating, "Maraming salamat! We made history nationwide! Grabe ang pasabog ng pagmamahal at paninindigan!"

The organization further recognized the collective effort of the community, expressing an ignited thank you to everyone who joined the catwalks, the marches, and the festivities under the #LOVELAB4N banner.

 "Sabay-sabay nating ipinakita ang lakas ng paggalaw nang sama-sama at patuloy na binigyang-buhay ang ating pambansang rampa," Pride PH added to emphasize the enduring power of unified social movements.

According to official crowd safety monitoring reports, the extensive security deployment ensured that the massive influx of civilian participants remained entirely peaceful despite heavy foot traffic and localized weather shifts throughout the day.

Furthermore, human rights organizers utilized the vast public platform to renew their legislative appeals to national lawmakers for the swift passage of a national SOGIE Equality Law and marriage equality bills.

Ultimately, independent economic analysts projected that the massive influx of regional visitors significantly boosted municipal tourism revenues through retail  markets, transport use, and hospitality services over the weekend.
